# TobyAI

Australian tax, super and payroll answers, computed and cited: the figure, the working and the section of the law it comes from.

TobyAI is for Australian business owners, their accounts and finance staff, and the accountants, bookkeepers and lawyers who advise them. Core covers payroll tax, PAYG withholding, super guarantee and Payday Super, GST and BAS, income tax, FBT, and termination and redundancy. Plus adds FairWork awards, a practice dashboard and scheduled audits; it is priced but not yet open for sign-up. The free Health Check reads a Xero file read-only and reports what is wrong and what to fix first.

Every figure is calculated by TobyAI's own software from published ATO, state revenue office and FairWork values, each with its source and the period it applies to. AI explains a result; it never sets a number. Customer data is processed and held in Sydney, and tax file numbers, bank details, salaries, staff names and organisation identifiers never reach AI.
