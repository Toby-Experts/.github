# TobyAI

Deterministic tax, super and payroll compliance for Australian bookkeepers, accountants and business owners.

TobyAI covers PAYG withholding, super guarantee and Payday Super, FBT, state payroll tax, and pay rates, allowances and penalty rates under FairWork modern awards, alongside a retained corpus of Commonwealth and state legislation, ATO guidance and rulings that can be read as at any date. Every figure TobyAI states is computed in auditable code from a versioned schedule of published ATO, FairWork Commission and state revenue office values, each carrying its source and the period it applies to. AI explains a computed result; it never originates a rate, threshold, date or legal position. All customer data is processed and stored in Sydney, and personal information is never sent to an AI provider.
