# Security policy

TobyAI is operated by Stelliot Investments Pty Ltd (ABN 81 127 096 851), trading as TobyAI.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email hello@tobyai.io with the subject line "Security report". Include the affected surface (www.tobyai.io, app.tobyai.io, api.tobyai.io or login.tobyai.io), steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed. Do not include customer data in the report.

We acknowledge reports within two business days (Sydney time) and keep you informed while we investigate. Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## Scope

In scope: the public site, the customer portal, the API, the free Xero health check and the sign-in surface listed above. Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering, and findings against third-party services we use (AWS, Netlify, Auth0, Stripe, Xero) that are not caused by our configuration.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research that stays within scope, avoids customer data and stops at the point of proof will not be pursued. We do not run a paid bounty programme.

## Data handling

Customer data is processed and stored in Sydney (AWS ap-southeast-2). Customer personal information is never sent to an AI provider. See https://www.tobyai.io/privacy.html.
